Digital microscope in 3D

Turn the model with one finger. Move the slider to take the parts apart. Tap a number to read about a part.

Tap a number to see the part name.

The AR model shows the same slider position. Set the slider first. Wait 2 seconds. Then tap the button.

Microscope view

Use the phone camera as a digital microscope. Hold the phone close to a small object.

Tap two points on the image to measure.
Note. This is digital zoom. A phone camera is not a real microscope. To get mm, tap two points on a ruler. Then tap Set scale and enter the true length. Keep the same distance to the object after you set the scale.

VR view

Put the phone in a VR holder. Each half shows the same camera image. This view is flat. It does not show true depth.

Turn the phone to landscape. On iPhone, full screen may not be available. Use the preview.

Help

How to see the microscope in your room
  1. Open this page in Chrome (Android) or Safari (iPhone or iPad).
  2. Go to the tab 3D and AR.
  3. Tap View in your room.
  4. Move the phone slowly over a flat surface.
  5. Tap the surface to put the model there.
If AR does not start
  • Do not open the link inside a chat or social app. Open it in the browser.
  • On Android, update Google Play Services for AR and Chrome.
  • Some phones do not support AR. Use the 3D view on those phones.
If the camera does not start
  • Allow camera access in the browser.
  • The page address must start with https.
  • Close other apps that use the camera.
