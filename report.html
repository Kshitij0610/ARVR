The digital microscope, taken apart

A digital microscope has a lens, a light and a camera. It sends the image to a screen. This sheet shows the 7 main parts.

SHEET 1 OF 4
THE DEVICE
Exploded view | side | not to scale
Parts list
  1. Base. It holds the microscope steady. It gives a flat surface for the sample.
  2. Post and focus knob. The post holds the head. Turn the knob to make the image sharp.
  3. Housing. It holds the head parts. It keeps out dust and stray light.
  4. USB board. It sends the video to a phone or a computer.
  5. Image sensor. It changes light into a digital image.
  6. Objective lens. It makes the object look larger.
  7. LED ring. It lights the object.

What we use it for

Six example uses. They are typical uses of a digital microscope. They are not our test results.

SHEET 2 OF 4
EXAMPLE USES
Use A

Circuit board check

Look at solder joints and thin tracks.

  • Find a bad solder joint early.
  • Check that chip pins touch the pads.
  • Save a photo for the quality record.
AR idea: show the pin names on the live image.
Use B

Metal surface and wear

Look at scratches, cracks and wear on metal parts.

  • Find small cracks on a machined surface.
  • Compare a worn part with a new part.
  • Measure the width of a scratch.
AR idea: show the allowed limit on the live image.
Use C

Biology and teaching

Show a leaf, an insect or a thin slice to a whole class.

  • The whole class sees the same image on a screen.
  • Students save photos for their report.
  • No eyepiece is necessary.
VR idea: students look at the sample in a VR holder.
Use D

Fabric and paper

Look at fibres and thread.

  • Count the threads in a fixed area.
  • Find a flaw in the weave.
  • Compare two types of fabric.
AR idea: show a grid on the live image.
Use E

Coins, ink and marks

Look at small marks on coins, ink and tools.

  • Read the small text on a coin.
  • Compare two ink lines.
  • Keep a dated photo record.
AR idea: share the live image with a remote expert.
Use F

Small parts and watch repair

Look at gears, springs and screws.

  • Find a bent tooth on a gear.
  • Check a part before you fit it.
  • Show the repair steps to a trainee.
VR idea: the trainee sees the repair in a VR holder.

Why AR and VR, and what comes next

AR adds digital objects to the real world. VR puts you in a computer world. Both make a digital microscope more useful.

SHEET 3 OF 4
THE PLAN
Reason 1

See it big

The image shows on a large screen or in a headset.

Reason 2

Add information

AR puts measurements and labels on the live image.

Reason 3

Share the view

A remote expert sees the same image as the user.

Reason 4

Train safely

Students practise on a safe sample. Nothing can break.

How the data moves
1Sample
2Lens and LED light
3Image sensor
4USB board
5Phone or computer
6AR overlay or VR holder
Proposed plan
  1. Phase 1. Web viewer with 3D, AR, camera and VR. This is done.
  2. Phase 2. Test with a real USB microscope. Set the scale with a ruler.
  3. Phase 3. Use two cameras for true stereo depth in VR.
  4. Phase 4. Test in a class. Ask students for feedback.

Scan and step inside

Point your phone camera at a code. Tap the link. You do not need to install an app.

SHEET 4 OF 4
SCAN
Code 1 | AR

See it in your room

  1. Scan the code.
  2. Tap View in your room.
  3. Move the phone slowly over a flat surface.
Code 2 | Camera

Use your camera as a microscope

  1. Scan the code.
  2. Tap Start camera. Allow access.
  3. Hold the phone close to a small object.
Code 3 | VR

Use the VR view

  1. Scan the code.
  2. Tap Start VR view.
  3. Put the phone in a VR holder.
AR

Augmented reality. Digital objects show in the real world, on the screen of your phone.

VR

Virtual reality. A computer world fills your view. Use a headset or a phone in a holder.

MR

Mixed reality. Digital objects react to the real room. This project does not use MR.

Caution. Use Chrome on Android or Safari on iPhone. Do not open the link inside a chat or social app. AR needs a phone that supports it. On Android, Google Play Services for AR must be installed.
