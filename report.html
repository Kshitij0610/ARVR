Overview

This report describes a 3D model and an AR and VR viewer for one digital microscope.

Numbers in Figure 1 match Table 1.

All-in-one zoom digital microscope

3D model, AR and VR viewer. Reference device: Steindorff S-VM701.

1.1Summary

A digital microscope has a lens, a camera and a light. It sends the image to a screen. This project helps people learn the device and share its image.

We made a 3D model of the S-VM701 from its datasheet and manual. A web page shows the model on any phone. You can take the model apart. You can also put it on a real table in AR.

The page has a camera demo and a VR view. You do not need to install an app.

2.1Software functions (built-in XCamView)

Image controls

Exposure (automatic or manual), gain, white balance (automatic, manual or ROI), sharpness, 3D denoise, saturation, contrast, brightness, gamma. Anti-flicker for 50 Hz and 60 Hz light.

Measurement

Point, line, angle, rectangle, circle, ellipse, annulus, polygon, curve, arc, scale bar, text and arrow. Calibrate with a micrometer. Export the results to a CSV file. Save the marks as a layer, or burn them into the image.

Display and files

Zoom in the video window up to 10x. Mirror, flip, freeze, color or gray, cross line. Browse images and play video from the SD card. Ten interface languages.

Detail views

Six close views of the 3D model.

Each view shows one group of datasheet facts.

Figure 3a. Camera ports

  • USB: connect a mouse to control the XCamView software.
  • HDMI: 1080p video at 60 fps (HDMI 1.4).
  • SD slot: stores JPEG images and MP4 video.
  • DC 12 V input and a status LED.

Figure 3b. Zoom lens body

  • Continuous zoom from 0.7x to 5.6x.
  • The body is 188 mm long and 52 mm wide.
  • With the 1.0x objective, NA is 0.018 to 0.092.
  • With the 1.0x objective, resolution is 18.6 µm to 3.65 µm.

Figure 3c. Auxiliary objective and LED ring

  • Five objectives: 0.5x, 0.75x, 1.0x, 1.5x and 2.0x.
  • The magnetic LED ring has no power cable.
  • Set the light with the wheel or with the software.
  • A polarizing ring light is also listed.
